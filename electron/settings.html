Settings

The app runs on this computer — your account, interviews, reports and recordings stay here. The AI interviewer, interviewer voices and speech recognition use your own API keys. Keys are encrypted with your Windows account and only sent to OpenAI / Anthropic.

AI keys

Create one at platform.openai.com/api-keys. Used for interviewer voices (gpt-4o-mini-tts), your answers' transcription (Whisper) and, if no Anthropic key is set, the interviewer (GPT-5.2).
With this key the interviewer and scoring use Claude Sonnet 4.5 (same as the website), with GPT-5.2 as fallback. Get one at console.anthropic.com.

Free to use

CyberHire is free. Every feature is unlocked. You only pay OpenAI / Anthropic directly for what your own key uses (a few cents per interview).
